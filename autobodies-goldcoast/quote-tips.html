Photo tips

How to photograph your vehicle

You can always ask for an on-site quote if you'd rather.

✕

Too far away

Don't shoot from so far that the damage is hard to see.

✕

Too close up

Include enough of the panel to show where the damage sits.

✕

Poor light

Avoid night, deep shade or low light.

✕

Blurry

Hold steady so the photo isn't blurred or distorted.

✕

Over-zoomed

Don't zoom in so far the image goes grainy.

✓

The ideal shot

Daylight, sharp, close enough to see detail, with a wider shot for context.
